# North Star

## Purpose

Help the church understand, design, implement, evaluate, and continually improve a church-wide culture and system of making disciples who make disciples.

## Desired person

An ordinary follower of Jesus who increasingly:

- knows and loves Jesus deeply;
- abides in Christ and practices the ways of Jesus;
- understands and lives according to Scripture;
- experiences genuine spiritual transformation;
- lives on mission in everyday relationships;
- shares the gospel and points others toward Jesus;
- intentionally helps another person grow as a disciple;
- equips that disciple to disciple others;
- takes increasing ownership of the mission of Jesus.

## Desired culture

Following Jesus naturally includes helping someone else follow Jesus. Staff equip people who equip people who equip people.

## Central question

> How can a local church create an environment in which increasing numbers of ordinary people become mature disciples of Jesus who intentionally make other disciples who eventually make other disciples?

## Non-goals

Attendance, knowledge, volunteering, programs, and group participation matter only as partial indicators/environments. None is sufficient evidence of mature or multiplying discipleship by itself.
