# Practice Approval Review — research to Dan's endorsed Southbrook design

## Purpose
Convert accumulated credible discipleship intelligence into **selective, reviewable pitches** and a durable record of Dan's choices. This is an approval/curation workflow, **not** another research cycle, a generic synthesis engine, or permission to launch church programs.

## Canonical ownership
- **GitHub**: this workflow, skills, pitch template, decision rules.
- **Drive Discipleship Brain**: evidence, Practice Cards, Living Synthesis, diagnostic, Dan's Pastoral Lens, existing `04 — DAN-SELECTED LEARNINGS & PRACTICES`, and the new review queue/approved-practice synthesis in the Brain.
- Never equate `Dan endorses exploring a practice` with `Southbrook leadership has adopted it`.
- Do not rewrite the empirically grounded Living Synthesis merely because Dan likes a practice. Living Synthesis changes only on independently assessed evidence under its own skill.

## Intake and review loop
`RELEVANT BRAIN EVIDENCE → DEDUPLICATE → EVALUATE (THEOLOGY / MECHANISM / SOURCES / LIMITS / LOCAL RELEVANCE) → SHORTLIST → PITCH TO DAN → EXPLICIT PER-ITEM CHOICE → RECORD SCOPE/RATIONALE → OPTIONAL TEST DESIGN → LOCAL LEARNING → RE-PITCH IF WARRANTED`

1. **Bound the review:** topic, real Southbrook problem, or a staged historical backlog. For whole-Brain backlog, inventory and work in meaningful source-family/topic batches; never claim full coverage after a sample.
2. **Read smallest sufficient context:** relevant Practice Intelligence index/cards, assessed evidence, existing Living Synthesis, Dan-selected learnings, Pastoral Lens, diagnostic/current constraints, prior decisions. Check duplicates and rejections.
3. **Evaluate** documented practice vs reported effect vs causal evidence vs mechanism support. Verify materially consequential claims at original source, or say "not yet verified." Count source *families*, not copies or AI restatements.
4. **Shortlist 3–5 worthwhile candidates** ranked by (a) potential to form/mobilize ordinary disciples, (b) North Star and Southbrook fit, (c) feasibility/reproducibility, (d) evidence quality/uncertainty, (e) freshness relative to approved ideas. Do not manufacture a pitch count; a batch may contain fewer.
5. **Pitch in normal language**, giving the essence, operational illustration, why it may work, strongest objection or risk, source(s) + locator, uncertainty, proposed approval scope, and recommended next action. Label `ASSISTANT PROPOSAL — NOT APPROVED`.
6. **Stop for Dan's selection.** Do not preemptively mark selected, approved, piloted, adopted, policy, or approved-synthesis entry.
7. **Record choice** for each ID with exact wording: `ENDORSE FOR DESIGN`, `APPROVE TO TEST`, `REVISE AND REPITCH`, `PARK`, `DECLINE`, or `NEEDS MORE EVIDENCE`. The first two describe Dan's personal pastoral design approval and, when clearly requested, approval to develop a test proposal—not authorization to conduct a test or adopt policy.
8. **Update canonical Drive records after explicit approval**: append approved-practice synthesis and maintain a decision history keyed by stable candidate ID; link original Practice Card and source; link the existing Dan-selected library only if personally selected resource learning fits it. A rejected/parked idea remains in review history but not the approved synthesis.
9. **Apply staff/pastoral guardrails**: the church's leadership must separately authorize commitments, program launches, theological policy, budgets and broad church-wide changes. Pilot design and execution are separate.
10. **Learning return:** after real local evidence exists, assess fidelity/outcomes and ask if the practice should be adapted, expanded, or dropped. Never treat mere approval as a proven outcome.

## Status architecture (four independent axes)
**Evidence confidence:** HIGH CONFIDENCE / PROMISING / CONTEXT DEPENDENT / UNCERTAIN / CONTRADICTED.
**Local action warrant:** EXPLORE / SMALL-TEST CANDIDATE / PILOT CANDIDATE / INTEGRATION CANDIDATE / SCALE CANDIDATE / NOT NOW.
**Dan review decision:** PENDING REVIEW / ENDORSE FOR DESIGN / APPROVE TO TEST / REVISE AND REPITCH / PARK / DECLINE / NEEDS MORE EVIDENCE.
**Church adoption:** NOT PROPOSED / AWAITING LEADERSHIP / AUTHORIZED FOR PILOT / ADOPTED / RETIRED; do not infer this from Dan's review decision.

## Approval record
Stable `APR-YYYY-###` or existing stable equivalent allocated against the live ledger; date; short title; domain; problem; source links and page/section; evidence classification and contradictions; mechanism; what practice actually looks like; boundary/risks; Dan's literal decision and scope; pastoral rationale where provided; recommended application; implementation state; review trigger; version/history. Check for collisions.

## Routing / stop rules
- New evidence → source checker / Practice Intelligence / Living Synthesis *only if warranted*.
- Choosing Southbrook practices → `southbrook-practice-fit-assessor`.
- Drafting a pilot only after Dan authorizes that deliverable → pilot designer.
- Writing review decisions → `discipleship-practice-approval-recorder`.
- Whole-corpus backfill is staged and deduplicated; do not crawl the entire Brain without a bounded scope or turn a request for pitches into a full new external research cycle.
- **Stop at the human gate.** Missing local diagnosis narrows confidence/approved scope; it does not justify invented current-state facts. A permission to keep an idea does not authorize action.
