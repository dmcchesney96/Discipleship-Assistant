# Research & Learning Workflow

## System loop

`SOURCE → INTAKE → VERIFY → ANALYZE → SYNTHESIZE → DIAGNOSE → DESIGN → MEASURE → EXPERIMENT → DECIDE → LEARN`

### 1. Source / Intake
Preserve provenance, classify, deduplicate, route. Do not prematurely conclude what the source proves.

### 2. Verify
Check important claims, citations, units, populations, dates, and independence. AI research remains input until verified.

### 3. Analyze
For churches/movements, identify definitions, pathway, practices, leadership system, multiplication mechanism, outcomes, failure modes, and evidence quality.

### 4. Synthesize
Update the living body of knowledge: convergences, disagreements, confidence levels, mechanisms, principles, gaps.

### 5. Diagnose Southbrook
Compare intention, delivered practice, and lived experience. Identify strengths, bottlenecks, perception gaps, and local constraints.

### 6. Design
Move from external practice to mechanism to principle to local application. Build toward one church-wide operating system, not isolated programs.

### 7. Measure
Layer evidence: access/pathway, participation/contribution, formation, disciple-making/multiplication, stories/pastoral discernment. Never turn engagement into a spiritual grade.

### 8. Experiment
Write a falsifiable hypothesis. Define people, practice, duration, support, measures, guardrails, and learning decision.

### 9. Decide
Prepare options/tradeoffs and identify the real decision-maker. Recommendation is not approval.

### 10. Learn
Preserve results, changed assumptions, decisions, and superseded models. Feed learning back into synthesis and current state.

## Multiplication discipline

Always ask: what is reproducing? Individual disciple-makers, leaders, groups, microchurches, churches, or plants? Track generations only within the same unit unless explicitly mapping transitions.
