## 1. Why this question matters

This work addresses an important uncertainty: whether an ordinary person's opportunity to explain what they have learned can be treated as proof of readiness to help another person follow Jesus independently. The packet supports learning about retention; it cannot answer whether a person abides in Christ, grows in character, lives on mission, makes a disciple, or equips that disciple to disciple someone else. Keeping those outcomes distinct protects the usefulness of educational evidence without turning it into a ministry mandate. [S1; S3]
