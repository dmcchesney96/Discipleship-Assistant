**What that means:** A chance to teach can be part of learning. It cannot serve as an automatic certificate of spiritual maturity or readiness to lead another person independently. The packet gives no evidence that two weeks is the right threshold. [S1; S3; S4]

**How sure we are:** CONTEXT DEPENDENT for educational benefit across the supplied conditions; transfer to church disciple reproduction remains UNCERTAIN. The packet contains one favorable study and one limiting study with different comparators. [S1; S3]

**Where this may not apply:** Adult science retention is not church formation. Unsupported novice explanation is not every form of supervised teaching. [S1; S3]

### Bounded claim

Preparing to teach and teaching may improve short-term educational retention under some conditions; unsupported novice explanation need not outperform retrieval practice, and transfer to spiritual maturity, independent leadership, or individual disciple reproduction remains unestablished by this evidence. [S1; S3]

### Meaning and limits

The claim concerns individual adult learners and educational retention/comparison performance. It does not make a claim about multiplied groups, churches, leaders, or generations. Teaching can be a learning activity; neither a correct explanation nor a completed lesson proves Christlike character, mission, or helping another person follow Jesus. The universal two-week independent leadership clause is removed from the research claim and retained in history as unsupported. D remains TENTATIVE, untried, and unapproved. [S1; S3; S4; S5]

### Practice, mechanism, principle, and rival explanation

Observed practice is preparation plus teaching in A, and unsupported novice explanation in C. Organizing, retrieval, and explanation could aid retention; repeating misconceptions could preserve error. These are mechanism candidates rather than measured mediators. The portable conclusion is conditional learning usefulness with separate outcome evidence required for ministry transfer. [S1; S3]

A plausible alternative explanation is the comparator: A compares against restudy whereas C compares against retrieval practice. Differences need not be due to supervision. The packet does not say A's teaching was supervised, and it does not experimentally isolate feedback. Do not rename A as proven supported teaching.
