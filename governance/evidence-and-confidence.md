# Evidence & Confidence Standard

## Evidence types

Every material claim should be classified when the distinction matters:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| DOCUMENTED_EVIDENCE | Identifiable source or local record supports the claim. |
| ORGANIZATIONAL_CLAIM | A church/ministry/network reports this about itself. |
| PRACTITIONER_OPINION | Experience-based judgment; useful but not causal proof. |
| INTERPRETATION | Reasoned synthesis from evidence. |
| HYPOTHESIS | Plausible proposition requiring verification/testing. |

For Southbrook evidence, also note whether it is a direct record, participant-reported experience, leader observation, approved decision, or proposal.

## Confidence language

- **HIGH CONFIDENCE** — convergent evidence strong enough to guide practice.
- **PROMISING** — positive signal with meaningful but incomplete support.
- **CONTEXT DEPENDENT** — direction plausibly varies by context/design/population.
- **UNCERTAIN** — evidence sparse, indirect, conflicting, or poorly verified.
- **CONTRADICTED** — a material claim conflicts with stronger evidence.

Confidence belongs to a claim in a defined scope, not to a source as a whole.

## Rules

1. Never treat marketing language as effectiveness evidence.
2. Never treat AI-generated research as final authority.
3. Never infer generational disciple multiplication from attendance, baptisms, groups launched, or churches planted unless the unit is explicitly connected.
4. Preserve contrary evidence and uncertainty.
5. Distinguish correlation, plausible mechanism, and demonstrated causation.
6. State population/time period/context for quantitative claims.
7. Treat movement-scale self-reports as organizational claims until independently supported.
8. Do not translate another source's confidence scale into this one without explaining the mapping.

## Evidence card

When useful, record:

- Claim
- Evidence type
- Source(s)
- Date/version
- Population/context
- Unit of analysis
- Strength/limitations
- Contrary evidence
- Project confidence
- Relevance to North Star
- Next verification step
