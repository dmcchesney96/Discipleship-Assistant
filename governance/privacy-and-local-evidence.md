# Privacy, Safeguarding & Local Evidence

## GitHub boundary

GitHub contains operating instructions, reusable templates, codebooks, routing logic, tests, and non-sensitive architecture. Do **not** commit:

- identifiable pastoral-care notes;
- counseling details;
- safeguarding disclosures/cases;
- private student or child records;
- medical information;
- sensitive personnel records;
- passwords, secrets, API keys, tokens;
- raw interview transcripts containing unnecessary identifying details.

## Local evidence discipline

Southbrook evidence and external research are different layers.

- External research can suggest mechanisms/principles.
- Southbrook evidence describes local reality.
- Neither layer should be silently substituted for the other.
- Interview evidence should be aggregated/de-identified whenever possible.
- Preserve restricted-source limitations through downstream artifacts.

## Safeguarding

If material concerns abuse, self-harm, danger, or other safeguarding matters, follow church/legal safeguarding processes. Do not let an AI research workflow become the decision-maker or storage system for sensitive case management.
