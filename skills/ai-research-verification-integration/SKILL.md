# AI Research Verification & Integration

## Mission

AI reports are **inputs, not authorities**. Preserve useful leads while preventing fabricated citations, misquoted studies, inflated movement claims, or unsupported synthesis from contaminating the Brain.

## Workflow

1. Register the AI report as AI-generated input with date/tool/source.
2. Extract:
   - important factual claims/statistics;
   - named studies/books/authors;
   - churches/networks/movements;
   - frameworks/models;
   - causal/mechanism claims;
   - practical recommendations.
3. Triage claims by importance and risk. Do not verify every trivial statement.
4. For high-value claims, locate the original or strongest accessible source.
5. Check citation existence, wording, date, population, denominator, unit, method, and whether the AI's conclusion is actually supported.
6. Compare with current Claims & Evidence, Living Synthesis, contradictions, and gaps.
7. Label each major claim:
   - VERIFIED / SUPPORTED;
   - PARTIALLY SUPPORTED;
   - UNVERIFIED;
   - MISLEADING / OVERSTATED;
   - CONTRADICTED;
   - SOURCE NOT FOUND.
8. Preserve useful verified primary sources in External Evidence; do not make the AI report the canonical evidence when the original is available.
9. Identify genuinely new insights versus repeated material.
10. Extract useful **practice leads** separately from effect claims. For a claimed church practice, verify the practice/process from primary materials when practical; do not require the same evidence to prove "they do this" as to prove "this caused the outcome."
11. Route supported synthesis changes to `living-discipleship-synthesis-maintainer`; practice leads/details to `discipleship-practice-intelligence-builder`; research gaps to the scout; specific organizations to case-study analysis.

## Multiplication checks

For any large multiplication claim, identify:

- who reported it;
- time period/geography;
- exact unit (individuals/groups/churches/etc.);
- generation definition;
- independent verification status;
- denominator/baseline where applicable.

## Output

**AI Research Verification Report**

- Report/source metadata
- High-value claims table
- Verification result + citations
- Errors/overclaims
- New credible sources recovered
- Agreement/contradiction with Brain
- Integrate / do not integrate / investigate further
- Practice leads worth preserving as Practice Intelligence
- Specific handoffs

Never silently “clean up” the AI report and present it as verified research.
