# Church & Movement Case Study Analyzer

## Core questions

### Definitions
How do they define disciple, discipleship, maturity, disciple-maker, multiplication, and success?

### Pathway
Map: `ENTRY → FORMATION → COMMUNITY → MISSION → LEADERSHIP → MULTIPLICATION`.

### Actual practice
Who disciples whom? What happens weekly/monthly? One-to-one, triads, groups, missional communities, coaching, Bible/prayer, evangelism, apprenticeships, family systems?

### Ordinary-person expectation
What is an ordinary participant expected and equipped to do? When do they begin practicing? What requires higher maturity/authority?

### Leadership system
How are disciple-makers identified, trained, observed, coached, released, corrected, and supported? How are leaders reproduced?

### Evangelism connection
How do people far from God enter relationships, hear/respond to gospel, and move into formation?

### Multiplication mechanism
What behavior/system plausibly causes another generation to emerge? Trace G0→G1→G2→G3→G4+ only when the unit is consistent and evidence supports it.

### Evidence
Separate:
- documented evidence;
- organizational claims;
- practitioner opinion;
- interpretation;
- hypothesis.

Look for independent corroboration, longitudinal persistence, internally developed leaders, reproducing disciple-makers, relational evangelism, group/leader reproduction, and sustained practices.

### Failure/limits
What has failed, stalled, been criticized, or depended on unique context? What theological, cultural, size, staffing, or movement conditions matter?

## Synthesis frame

For every notable practice:

`PRACTICE → OBSERVED OUTCOME → PLAUSIBLE MECHANISM → TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE → LOCAL APPLICATION OPTIONS`

Do not conclude “Church X does this, so Southbrook should.”

## Output

- Case snapshot/context
- Definitions
- Pathway/system map
- Practices and leadership/coaching system
- Multiplication mechanism
- Evidence matrix with confidence
- Failure modes/risks
- Transferable principles
- Context-specific practices
- Southbrook questions/hypotheses (not prescriptions)
- Research gaps / sources
