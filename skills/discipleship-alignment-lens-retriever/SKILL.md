# Discipleship Alignment Lens Retriever

## Purpose

Make the Discipleship Assistant increasingly understand how Dan thinks, not merely what the research says.

Before substantial creation or revision work, retrieve the relevant stored pastoral convictions and design preferences so the output can fit Dan's ministry philosophy without pretending those preferences are evidence.

## Trigger

Use automatically before substantial work involving:

- discipleship strategy;
- church-wide system/pathway design;
- Bible-study curriculum;
- discipleship curriculum;
- facilitator/participant materials;
- leadership development;
- family/student/kids discipleship design;
- experiments/pilots;
- measurement design where values affect what is measured;
- major revisions where Dan has previously given qualitative feedback about what feels right/wrong.

Do not use for simple factual questions unless the user's alignment materially affects the answer.

## Retrieval rule

Retrieve the smallest sufficient set.

### For Bible study / curriculum

Retrieve:

1. the relevant portions of `02 — Bible & Curriculum Design Philosophy`;
2. relevant convictions from `01 — Living Discipleship Convictions`;
3. directly related unresolved tensions from `04` only when they could change the design.

### For strategy / systems / implementation

Retrieve:

1. relevant portions of `03 — Ministry Strategy & System Preferences`;
2. relevant convictions from `01`;
3. directly related `04` items where unresolved.

### For research design

Use the lens only to understand the ministry question, desired outcome, and local values.

Do not use it to predetermine findings or downgrade inconvenient evidence.

## Priority and conflict rules

Use this order:

1. current explicit user instruction;
2. Scripture / responsible theological interpretation and approved doctrinal boundaries;
3. current approved Southbrook decisions/policies;
4. current local evidence and constraints;
5. relevant `USER ALIGNMENT / PASTORAL LENS`;
6. external evidence/practice as appropriate to the task;
7. historical or superseded preferences.

This ordering is not a claim that preferences outrank evidence. It means the lens is a design input once truth, authority, and current constraints are respected.

When a stored preference conflicts with stronger evidence, an approved decision, or the user's current instruction, surface the tension rather than silently choosing one.

## Output

Return a compact alignment packet:

- relevant foundational/working convictions;
- relevant design preferences;
- explicit "NOT FOR US" constraints;
- unresolved/tentative questions;
- design implications;
- any meaningful conflict with evidence/current decisions.

Label this packet:

`USER ALIGNMENT / PASTORAL LENS`

Do not call it research evidence.

## Success test

The user should be able to keep talking naturally over time, and future substantial work should increasingly sound like it understands his actual ministry philosophy without requiring him to restate the same preferences in every prompt.
