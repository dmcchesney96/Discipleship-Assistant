# Discipleship Practice Approval Pitch Builder

Read `docs/practice-approval-review-workflow.md` and `templates/practice-approval-pitch-template.md`.

## Trigger
- "What should we approve/use from our research?"
- "Pitch me good practices from the Brain."
- "What have we learned that deserves my approval?"
- A substantive new research/synthesis pass has genuinely actionable candidate(s) and the user wants ongoing review pitches.

## Procedure
1. Define relevant discipleship outcome/local problem; scope past research to the smallest useful set. If historical backfill is requested, work in explicit batches and show coverage gap.
2. Retrieve live Brain evidence, Practice Intelligence/cards, current principles, Dan's Pastoral Lens and selected resources, relevant diagnostic, pending/rejected ledger; check sources and duplicate practice families.
3. Identify 3–5 candidates (or fewer), select *not previously approved*, assess against Scripture/theology, evidence, operational specificity, coaching, ordinary-person reproducibility, staff dependence, risk and transferability. Separate documented practice, outcome claim, effect evidence, mechanism support, interpretation, hypothesis. No scoring theater.
4. Identify strongest counterargument, a credible alternative, conditions where it might not work, and what local evidence is missing.
5. Pitch each with a stable provisional ID, 90–180 words max unless asked to go deeper. Start with what ordinary people actually do; give source locator, confidence, specific Southbrook context and a small next step. Highlight if original primary source has not been independently verified.
6. Present no more than five in each review conversation. Ask for per-item decisions in plain language. Keep in PENDING REVIEW until user decides.
7. If user gives choices, hand off to `discipleship-practice-approval-recorder`. Never record an assistant recommendation as Dan's endorsement.

## Output
A short introduction stating scope; candidate pitches using template; one review question. Label the whole board `PROPOSALS — AWAITING DAN'S APPROVAL`. State what corpus is unreviewed and avoid claiming complete Brain-wide mining from a selected batch.

## Important
Research principle promotion is governed by Living Synthesis evidence rules, not by pastoral preference. Personal approval is not church policy or approval to launch a pilot. Respect documented previous declines; only repitch if new evidence materially changes warrant, or Dan requests reconsideration.
