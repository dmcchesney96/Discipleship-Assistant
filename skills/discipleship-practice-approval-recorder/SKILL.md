# Discipleship Practice Approval Recorder

Read `docs/practice-approval-review-workflow.md`.

## Trigger
Dan explicitly approves, rejects, revises, parks, or requests verification of a pitched APR idea, or asks to revisit his approved-practice synthesis.

## Work
1. Identify exact APR ID(s) and scope of user's words. Approval of a principle, a resource, a test *design*, and a **church policy** are separate; do not broaden.
2. Retrieve live Drive review ledger, approved synthesis, related Practice Card and relevant prior Dan-selected record. Detect duplicates, contradictions and current revisions.
3. Record Dan's words verbatim when available, date, scope, rationale and decision, preserving previous state in history.
4. For `ENDORSE FOR DESIGN` or `APPROVE TO TEST`, update **Dan-Approved Southbrook Design Practices — Living Selection Synthesis** with a concise entry: why selected, practice, principle/mechanism, source links, evidence caveats, suggested use, next step, re-review trigger. If explicitly a source learning, also cross-link existing Dan-selected library; don't create a duplicate record.
5. For `REVISE AND REPITCH` / `PARK` / `DECLINE` / `NEEDS MORE EVIDENCE`, update review ledger only and preserve the reason. No approved-synthesis insertion.
6. If user later reverses, mark prior approval SUPERSEDED/RETIRED in record and remove from active approved index without erasing history.
7. Update Living Synthesis **only** if new assessed evidence materially supports an evidence-based principle, routed to `living-discipleship-synthesis-maintainer`. User endorsement alone cannot raise evidence confidence.
8. If test design is approved, route design to pilot skill; do not launch or commit staff, budgets, communications or church policy without separate authorization.
9. Verify native Drive readback (body and parent), stable ID/index status, and return precise links and decision scope. If a write fails, say what is saved and what isn't; don't claim completion.

## Non-negotiables
- Never infer approval from silence or an upbeat comment.
- Preserve Dan's selected wording; avoid invented quotes.
- No church-wide "adopted" label without evidence of the actual authorized church decision.
- Avoid pastoral/student sensitive information in GitHub or cross-church summaries.
