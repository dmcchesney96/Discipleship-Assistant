# Discipleship Research Scout & Agenda Builder

## Purpose

Decide **what should be researched next and why**, not conduct the full research.

## Inputs

- Living Synthesis
- Research Intelligence / gaps / verification queue
- Southbrook Diagnostic
- active experiments/strategy questions
- recent incoming reports
- known upcoming decisions

## Gap types

Do not treat every unknown as an empirical-research gap. Classify candidate work as one of:

- **TRUTH / EVIDENCE GAP** — what appears true is materially uncertain;
- **MECHANISM GAP** — we see a pattern/practice but do not understand why it may work;
- **PRACTICE INTELLIGENCE GAP** — we know the principle but lack concrete operational examples of how credible practitioners implement it;
- **TRANSFERABILITY GAP** — we do not know what depends on context;
- **SOUTHBROOK FIT GAP** — the external knowledge is adequate but local diagnosis/fit is unclear;
- **IMPLEMENTATION GAP** — we know what may fit but not how to test/coach/measure it.

Route practice/fit/implementation gaps to the appropriate practice-design skill instead of reflexively launching a literature review.

## Workflow

1. Map what is already well-covered versus weakly covered.
2. Identify unresolved contradictions and high-leverage unknowns.
3. Separate curiosity from decision-relevant research.
4. Score candidate questions qualitatively on:
   - North Star importance;
   - novelty / redundancy risk;
   - decision relevance;
   - expected evidence availability;
   - transferability;
   - urgency;
   - value of resolving uncertainty.
5. Prefer questions that could change a decision, sharpen a mechanism, test a popular assumption, or close a major evidence gap.
6. Sequence dependencies: foundational definitions/evidence before downstream implementation details.
7. Recommend the most suitable method: literature review, primary-source audit, case study, comparison, historical/global scan, **practice-intelligence scan**, implementation study, local diagnostic, fit assessment, pilot, or verification sprint.
8. When a principle is already reasonably established but practical implementation is thin, prefer a practice-intelligence question over another abstract evidence review.

## Output

**Research Agenda**

- Top priority question
- Why now / what it could change
- What is already known
- Gap type
- Specific evidence/practice/fit gap
- Recommended method/source types
- Success criteria
- 3–7 secondary questions
- Questions intentionally deferred + why

Do not launch the research automatically unless the caller requested a multi-step workflow.
