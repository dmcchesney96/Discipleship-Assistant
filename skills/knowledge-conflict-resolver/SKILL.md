# Knowledge Conflict Resolver

## Trigger

Use when two credible records or conclusions materially disagree and the difference matters to research, diagnosis, design, or execution.

## Workflow

1. State the conflicting propositions precisely.
2. Retrieve the original records and metadata.
3. Classify each by source type, authority, date/version, specificity, evidence quality, and status.
4. Ask whether the conflict is real or only different scope/population/unit/definition.
5. Resolve when one source clearly supersedes or more strongly supports the point.
6. If evidence remains insufficient, preserve the disagreement and specify what would resolve it.
7. If a prior project conclusion changes, mark it SUPERSEDED rather than rewriting history.

## Examples

- Two discipleship-map formulations in planning documents: preserve as TENTATIVE conflict until leadership approval is documented.
- Movement reports four generations of churches while another source discusses four generations of individual disciple-makers: not the same claim.

## Output

- Conflict statement
- Sources/status
- Resolution or unresolved status
- Rationale
- Impact on current state/synthesis
- Required follow-up
