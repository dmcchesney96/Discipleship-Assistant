# Living Discipleship Synthesis Maintainer

## Purpose

Make the project increasingly smarter as research accumulates. This skill updates project-level mechanisms/principles, not raw evidence **and not the catalog of concrete ministry practices**.

Concrete external practices belong in the Practice Intelligence Library unless the evidence supports a more general mechanism/principle.

## Preconditions

Use assessed/verified evidence. Do not promote an AI report, single organizational claim, or isolated anecdote directly into a high-confidence principle.

## Workflow

1. Identify which synthesis domain is affected.
2. Retrieve current synthesis plus the new assessed evidence.
3. State what the synthesis currently says and its confidence.
4. Compare new evidence:
   - confirms;
   - strengthens;
   - narrows;
   - complicates;
   - contradicts;
   - does not materially change.
5. Track independent convergence and meaningful disagreement.
6. Separate universal/portable principles from context-specific practices.
7. Update mechanisms before programs: Practice → Mechanism → Principle.
8. Apply confidence language from `AGENTS.md` with rationale.
9. Record what evidence would change the conclusion.
10. Preserve previous conclusions/version history when changed.
11. Add open questions/research gaps.

## Core synthesis domains

Maturity/definition; formation; abiding/practices; community/accountability; obedience/character; mission/evangelism; disciple-making; generational multiplication; apprenticeship/coaching; leadership development; family; student/child; culture; systems/pathways; measurement/transformation.

## Output

**Synthesis Update**

- Domain
- Prior conclusion/confidence
- New evidence
- Updated conclusion/confidence
- Mechanism/principle
- Supporting and contrary evidence
- Context limits
- Southbrook implication as hypothesis/question
- Open research
- Version/change note

A synthesis update is not a ministry decision.

When new evidence mainly adds a useful **how-to practice** rather than changing a project-level conclusion, route it to `discipleship-practice-intelligence-builder` instead of forcing it into Living Synthesis.

## Durable-principle contract (required)

Use `templates/durable-principle-record-template.md` and `docs/principle-synthesis-system.md` for every new/changed durable principle. A synthesis document may contain multiple records, but every record must remain independently traceable.

1. Search the topic's existing principle records and the Master Principles Index *before* creating an ID or new record. Prefer updating an existing principle over a near-duplicate.
2. Give the principle one stable `PR-###` ID allocated from the live index (or preserve its pre-existing stable identifier and register an alias). Never silently renumber, recycle, or invent an ID that collides with another record.
3. Preserve a concise human-readable **claim**, its **scope and outcome**, **candidate mechanism**, the **practice-to-mechanism distinction**, and **what the claim does not mean**.
4. Record a claim-level evidence map: original source title/author/date and stable Drive/primary URL, page/location, exact supported proposition, type of support (direct, mechanism, practice, reported outcome, interpretation), context, and source-family ID. Cross-referenced summaries of one study count once; independent primary studies count separately. Note contrary evidence and missing evidence.
5. Separate **evidence strength for mechanism**, **direct disciple-making outcome evidence**, and **project conclusion confidence**. If there is no direct church-outcome evidence, say so plainly; do not inherit an AI report's confidence label.
6. Require a specific **promotion decision**: NEW DURABLE PRINCIPLE / STRENGTHENS / NARROWS / COMPLICATES / CONTRADICTS / NO DURABLE CHANGE / NEW LEAD. Only first five can change a durable record, and only when assessed evidence warrants it. Hypotheses and attractive unverified ideas remain Research Intelligence, not high-confidence synthesis.
7. State counterexamples, boundary conditions, an alternative plausible explanation, what evidence could change the conclusion, and appropriate Southbrook implications (questions/hypotheses, never automatic church policy).
8. Add version date, prior wording/confidence, what changed and why, source links and review trigger. Mark genuinely replaced claims SUPERSEDED; keep the history.
9. On authorized writes, update the topic synthesis and Master Principles Index together, verifying both links and avoiding duplicate rows. If either write is blocked, mark INCOMPLETE / INDEX OUT OF SYNC; do not claim completion.
10. Respect privacy: only aggregate/deidentified local data enters a cross-church principle. Keep local findings and proposed practice decisions in their owning Brain layers.

### Quality gate — reject promotion if

- the source is solely an unverified AI narrative or one church marketing claim;
- three AI reports repeat one primary study but are counted as three independent confirmations;
- the claim confuses individual disciples with groups, leaders, or planted churches;
- the supported source finding concerns learning/participation but the principle states proven G3/G4 multiplication;
- contrary evidence or source limitations have been omitted;
- a specific practice or proposed Southbrook decision is mislabeled as a portable universal principle;
- a near-duplicate principle already exists and no merge/update was attempted;
- source locators, confidence rationale, or previous version are missing for a material change.

### Recovery before another write

If a prior topic update succeeded but its index write failed, reread both records and repair the existing ID's stale index before adding any new record. Preserve the successful topic version and the failure history. Record an operation key (principle ID + intended version + source-set fingerprint), observed versions, write results, and readback. A repeat of a completed operation is NO CHANGE; if the live record changed since preparation, reconcile before writing. Where version guards are available, use them. If safe conflict checking is unavailable, defer the conflicting write and return a reviewable draft.

### Write result

Return principle IDs and direct Drive links; NEW/UPDATED/NO CHANGE; the source-family count; confidence shift; open evidence gaps; index-sync status; and the reason for stopping. Never manufacture a required number of principles.

### Counterevidence protection

Log counterevidence explicitly beside evidence favoring the claim; avoid relegating substantive disconfirmation to a vague note or silently lowering its significance. Describe how the best contrary source would change wording, confidence, scope or status.

## Write learning people can understand

Lead each update with the useful learning in ordinary language before the technical record.

For every changed conclusion provide:
1. **What we learned:** one short sentence with a person and an action.
2. **What that means:** two or three plain sentences.
3. **What it could look like:** a concrete everyday ministry example.
4. **What someone could try:** a small action or question, clearly labeled as a proposal when appropriate.
5. **How sure we are:** the existing confidence label plus one sentence explaining it.
6. **Where this may not apply:** the important limit or contrary finding.

Prefer "Let them make a real decision, then talk through how it went" to "Transfer reproductive ownership through graduated responsibility." Prefer "Check whether the person they taught can do it too" to "Assess downstream generational fidelity." Explain a necessary specialist term once in familiar words; do not make the reader decode jargon.

Apply a read-aloud check: could an ordinary volunteer explain this to another volunteer without translating it? Revise abstract noun stacks, vague slogans and unexplained labels until they can. Keep the source map, stable IDs, evidence distinctions, confidence, counterevidence and version history after the readable learning. Never simplify away a material caveat, a theological boundary or the difference between a proposed practice and an approved decision.

Do not rewrite unrelated historical records for style. Improve the affected current learning while preserving prior wording in history.
