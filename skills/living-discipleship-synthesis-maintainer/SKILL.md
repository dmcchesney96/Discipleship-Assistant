# Living Discipleship Synthesis Maintainer

## Purpose

Make the project increasingly smarter as research accumulates. This skill updates project-level mechanisms/principles, not raw evidence **and not the catalog of concrete ministry practices**.

Concrete external practices belong in the Practice Intelligence Library unless the evidence supports a more general mechanism/principle.

## Preconditions

Use assessed/verified evidence. Do not promote an AI report, single organizational claim, or isolated anecdote directly into a high-confidence principle.

## Workflow

1. Identify which synthesis domain is affected.
2. Retrieve current synthesis plus the new assessed evidence.
3. State what the synthesis currently says and its confidence.
4. Compare new evidence:
   - confirms;
   - strengthens;
   - narrows;
   - complicates;
   - contradicts;
   - does not materially change.
5. Track independent convergence and meaningful disagreement.
6. Separate universal/portable principles from context-specific practices.
7. Update mechanisms before programs: Practice → Mechanism → Principle.
8. Apply confidence language from `AGENTS.md` with rationale.
9. Record what evidence would change the conclusion.
10. Preserve previous conclusions/version history when changed.
11. Add open questions/research gaps.

## Core synthesis domains

Maturity/definition; formation; abiding/practices; community/accountability; obedience/character; mission/evangelism; disciple-making; generational multiplication; apprenticeship/coaching; leadership development; family; student/child; culture; systems/pathways; measurement/transformation.

## Output

**Synthesis Update**

- Domain
- Prior conclusion/confidence
- New evidence
- Updated conclusion/confidence
- Mechanism/principle
- Supporting and contrary evidence
- Context limits
- Southbrook implication as hypothesis/question
- Open research
- Version/change note

A synthesis update is not a ministry decision.

When new evidence mainly adds a useful **how-to practice** rather than changing a project-level conclusion, route it to `discipleship-practice-intelligence-builder` instead of forcing it into Living Synthesis.
