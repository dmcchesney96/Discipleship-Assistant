# Source & Evidence Checker

## Purpose

Prevent attractive claims from outrunning their evidence.

## Workflow

1. State the exact claim being evaluated.
2. Identify source type: primary organizational, independent research, peer-reviewed, survey, practitioner, AI-generated, local interview/data, etc.
3. Inspect provenance, date, population, sample, method, comparator, outcome definition, and unit of analysis.
4. Distinguish what the source directly establishes from inference.
5. Check whether numerical claims have a clear denominator and whether generational multiplication claims specify the reproducing unit.
6. Identify independence/conflicts of interest, self-report limitations, missing data, and contrary evidence.
7. Assess transferability to Southbrook separately from truth of the original claim.
8. Assign project confidence only when enough evidence exists.
9. Keep **claim confidence** separate from **action warrant**. Limited direct evidence for an exact practice does not automatically mean a low-risk reversible test is unwise; that judgment belongs to `southbrook-practice-fit-assessor` and depends on theology, mechanism, precedent, local fit, risk, and reversibility.

## Access and claim support

Record the exact locator and access depth for each checked source. A search snippet or abstract may establish a narrow proposition; label everything requiring unavailable methods/results as unverified. For numbers, preserve the exact comparison, denominator, time window and outcome; do not infer an incremental effect by subtracting effect sizes from different comparisons.

## Evidence strength

Use descriptive strength (Strong / Moderate / Limited) for source support where helpful, and project confidence vocabulary from `AGENTS.md` for synthesized conclusions. Do not silently map one to the other.

## Output

- Claim
- What the source supports
- What it does not support
- Source/method assessment
- Evidence strength
- Limitations/contrary evidence
- Project-confidence implication
- Verification needs
- Transferability note
- Action-warrant note: what this evidence assessment can/cannot say about trying the practice locally; route to `southbrook-practice-fit-assessor` when needed
