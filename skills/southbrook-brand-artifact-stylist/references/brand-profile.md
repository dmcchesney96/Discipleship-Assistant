## Typography working rule

### Professional / outward-facing design

**Gotham family — HIGH CONFIDENCE working precedent.**

Use Gotham when:

- the authorized production environment has a licensed Gotham installation;
- the artifact is being professionally designed or exported from an environment where Gotham is legitimately available;
- a current Communications template already contains Gotham.

Do not redistribute, extract, or package Gotham font files.

Do not call Gotham the formally published "official Southbrook font" unless Communications or a formal brand guide explicitly confirms that wording.

### Editable internal documents

Use **Arial** as the safe editable-document fallback when Gotham is unavailable.

Recent internal Southbrook ministry documents use Arial extensively.

Practical letter-size hierarchy:

- title: 22–28 pt bold, charcoal;
- subtitle: 10–12 pt italic, gray;
- section label: 9–11 pt bold, often uppercase cyan;
- body: 9–11 pt charcoal;
- table header: bold white on cyan;
- footer: 7–9 pt gray.

Arial is a production fallback, not the primary professional-design identity font.

## Working layout grammar

Southbrook ministry artifacts should generally feel:

- modern;
- clean;
- direct;
- spacious;
- practical;
- high-contrast;
- lightly branded rather than heavily decorated.

Prefer:

- generous negative space;
- clean rectangular geometry;
- thin cyan rules;
- uppercase or clearly differentiated category labels;
- strong typographic hierarchy;
- concise callouts;
- practical tables;
- photography or expressive design only when the specific event/series calls for it.

Avoid by default:

- ornate church imagery;
- generic Christian visual tropes;
- decorative script fonts as the default;
- gradients without a specific design reason;
- oversized decorative icons;
- excessive cyan;
- consumer-app rounded-card aesthetics;
- treating one event palette as the church-wide palette.
