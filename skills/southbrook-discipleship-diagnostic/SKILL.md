# Southbrook Discipleship Diagnostic

## Mission

Answer: **What is actually happening now, what is producing fruit, where are people getting stuck, and what local conditions matter?**

Diagnose before designing.

## Evidence layers

1. **Intended** — official mission, pathway, teaching, ministry design, role expectations.
2. **Delivered** — what leaders/teams actually do repeatedly.
3. **Experienced** — what adults, parents, students, volunteers, newcomers report living.
4. **Observed outcomes** — data, practices, stories, leadership reproduction, mission, disciple-making/multiplication.

## Diagnostic domains

- definition/clarity of disciple and maturity;
- gospel/identity and abiding;
- Scripture/prayer/spiritual practices;
- obedience/character/transformation;
- community/accountability/relational depth;
- mission/evangelism/hospitality/service;
- intentional disciple-making;
- generational multiplication;
- parent/family ownership;
- student/child ownership;
- leadership pipeline/apprenticeship/coaching;
- pathway/next-step clarity;
- ministry handoffs/transitions;
- program/staff dependency;
- culture: language, stories, celebration, modeling, expectations, measures;
- cross-ministry alignment;
- barriers/capacity/context.

## Workflow

1. Set scope/as-of date and evidence coverage.
2. Use current-state synthesis for authoritative local systems/decisions.
3. Integrate qualitative interview analysis, local data, ministry docs, and direct observations.
4. Build a ministry-by-domain matrix.
5. Identify strengths to preserve, not only problems.
6. Identify bottlenecks/pathway breaks and who is affected.
7. Identify leader-vs-participant perception gaps.
8. Look for cross-ministry patterns and duplicated/contradictory systems.
9. Separate evidence from interpretation and causal hypothesis.
10. Compare to external research only after the local reality is clear.
11. Frame gaps as testable problems, not predetermined programs.

## Output

**Current Reality Diagnostic**

- Executive summary
- Evidence coverage/limitations
- Strongest pockets of health
- Whole-church patterns
- Ministry-specific findings
- Pathway/bottleneck map
- Culture analysis
- Leadership/multiplication analysis
- Parent/student/intergenerational findings
- Perception gaps
- What is known / inferred / unknown
- Priority diagnostic questions
- Candidate hypotheses worth testing
