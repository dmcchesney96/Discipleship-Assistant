## What is directly documented

The website consistently labels the public weekly artifact **Message Notes**.

On weeks where both are available, **Message Notes** and **Study Questions** are separate downloads. This distinction is visible on the Grudges, Greed, and Gossip sermon pages.

Across the sampled Message Notes, the extracted content repeatedly begins with:

1. series + sermon title;
2. primary Scripture passage;
3. speaker + date.

Examples:

- `THINK BIBLICALLY: RESPECTABLE SINS | GRUDGES`
- `Romans 12:19-21`
- `Bradley Caffee | 9.27.2026`

and

- `Revival | PRIORITIZING WORSHIP`
- `Ezra 3-4`
- `Shane Freeman | 8.2.2026`

## Stable content grammar

### 1. Sermon metadata leads

The handout opens with the sermon identity, not a generic Southbrook title page.

Stable metadata:

- series;
- sermon title;
- main passage;
- speaker;
- date.

### 2. The notes follow the sermon argument

The handout is not merely a synopsis.

Sampled notes preserve:

- the sermon's movements;
- key claims;
- key definitions;
- important diagnostic questions;
- biblical support;
- closing application.

### 3. Section headings are argument-driven

Observed headings include:

- `Defining Grudges Biblically`
- `The Downward Spiral of Anger`
- `Releasing a Grudge`
- `Initial Thoughts about Greed`
- `Thinking Biblically about Greed`
- `Taking Personal Inventory`
- `Why Respectable Sins?`
- `Thinking Biblically about Gossip`
- `Putting a Stop to Gossip`
- `The Character of a Spiritual Leader`
- `The Priority of Worship`

Interpretation: Southbrook's notes make the preacher's logical movement visible rather than forcing standardized headings such as Point 1 / Point 2 / Point 3.

### 4. Key points are complete statements

Observed point forms include:

- `Greed isn't just about money.`
- `Greed reveals our greatest loves.`
- `Gossip isn't really about facts - it's about the heart.`
- `Trust in the Sovereignty of God`
- `Spiritual leaders are lifelong students of God's Word`
- `Nothing matters more than our relationship with God`

Interpretation: the artifact favors memorable propositions and actions over isolated keywords.

### 5. Scripture is woven into the outline

Scripture references repeatedly sit directly before or after the point they support.

The notes sometimes include only the reference and sometimes include the full passage text.

Long central texts can occupy substantial space. Scripture density is not treated as a problem to eliminate.

### 6. Different point systems are allowed

Examples use:

- bullet points;
- numbered points;
- short lists;
- sequences / arrows;
- application questions.

Therefore the reusable artifact should preserve the speaker's point logic rather than impose a single list style.

### 7. Application is still part of Message Notes

Message Notes can end with a response movement without becoming a Study Questions document.

Observed forms:

- `Taking Personal Inventory` with personal questions;
- `Putting a Stop to Gossip` with response actions;
- final `How is God prompting you?` response sequence;
- forgiveness/action steps.

### 8. These are complete notes, not fill-in-the-blank worksheets

In the six sampled 2026 examples, the public notes contain complete statements and Scripture rather than obvious missing-word blanks.

Working rule: do **not** convert the weekly Southbrook Message Notes into a fill-in-the-blank format unless a current sermon source explicitly uses one.

## Cross-series variation

The exact header treatment and point presentation change between series.

`Revival` examples use a series/title construction that extracts differently from the later `Think Biblically: Respectable Sins` notes.

Working interpretation:

- the **content grammar** is stable;
- series identity is flexible;
- current series artwork/title styling should be allowed to change;
- general Southbrook branding should support, not erase, the series visual language.

## Strongest reusable pattern

`SERIES | SERMON TITLE`

`PRIMARY PASSAGE`

`SPEAKER | DATE`

`optional series/big-idea framing sentence`

`SECTION / MOVEMENT`

`key sermon point`

`Scripture reference`

`quoted Scripture when central`

`next key sermon point`

`...`

`FINAL RESPONSE / APPLICATION MOVEMENT`

## What this source set does NOT verify

These public PDF extractions do **not** by themselves establish:

- an official sermon-notes font family;
- exact margin dimensions;
- exact logo placement or logo presence;
- formal page-count requirements;
- a required Southbrook cyan treatment;
- a fixed number of sermon points;
- a fixed Scripture translation.

Those items must come from the current series design, an official brand source, or direct visual inspection of the source PDF. Do not invent them.

## Confidence

### HIGH CONFIDENCE

- Message Notes are a distinct weekly public artifact.
- Header metadata order is highly consistent.
- Notes follow sermon movements and preserve substantial Scripture.
- Complete statements are normal.
- Application can be included.
- Study Questions are separate when published.

### PROMISING / WORKING INFERENCE

- The most transferable template is content-structural rather than visually rigid.
- Series-specific design should sit on top of a stable notes grammar.

### UNCERTAIN

- exact visual page grid;
- exact fonts;
- exact logo usage;
- exact spacing and page-number conventions.
