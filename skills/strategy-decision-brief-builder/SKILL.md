# Strategy & Decision Brief Builder

## Purpose

Help a real decision-maker choose a faithful, feasible next step.

## Inputs

Decision question; audience/decision authority; deadline if known; current approved direction; local constraints/capacity; relevant diagnostic, synthesis, pilot, and measurement findings; requested format.

## Workflow

1. Frame the decision in one sentence and why it matters now. Distinguish an information brief from a request for decision.
2. Build the evidence spine: current local reality, strongest applicable research, pilot results, constraints, conflict, uncertainty.
3. Keep Southbrook evidence separate from external applicability and theological commitments.
4. Define genuinely feasible options. Include maintain-current-practice or smaller reversible test when plausible; avoid straw options.
5. Compare options on:
   - North Star contribution;
   - ordinary-person reproducibility;
   - local fit;
   - relational/theological depth;
   - capacity/cost;
   - evidence;
   - time to learn;
   - dependencies/risks;
   - reversibility/opportunity cost.
6. Recommend an option only when requested/appropriate, with confidence and what would change the recommendation.
7. Distinguish immediate experiments, 3–6 month pilots, one-year changes, and multi-year culture.
8. Specify the exact decision requested, prerequisites, next steps, measures, and review trigger.
9. Owners/dates/funding are confirmed only when evidenced; suggestions are labeled proposed.
10. After a real decision, route to `decision-action-extractor` and preserve durable rationale through `institutional-memory-writer`.

## Output

**Decision Brief — [Question]**

- Status: Proposed / Information / Decided (only if documented)
- Recommendation or information takeaway
- Decision needed + decision-maker
- Why now
- Current reality
- Evidence/confidence
- Options/tradeoffs
- Risks/unknowns
- Capacity/cost assumptions
- Proposed implementation/pilot
- Measures/review trigger
- Next actions
- Sources

Destination: `10 — STRATEGY & DECISIONS`, with drafts in Agent Workspace when appropriate.
