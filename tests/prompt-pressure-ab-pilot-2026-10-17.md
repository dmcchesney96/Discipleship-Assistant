# Prompt-pressure A/B pilot: supervised apprentice coaching

**Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Status:** ONE-SESSION UNBLINDED PILOT · **Not a passing independent model evaluation**

## Same research gap
For adult student-ministry volunteers, how should supervised practice, specific feedback, and mentor-coaching dose be balanced to develop independently demonstrated, safe responsibility—and eventually an ability to guide a next adult volunteer—without overburdening mentors?

## Brain starting point
The existing *Apprenticeship Before Solo Leadership* Practice Card calls apprenticeship PROMISING but says optimal dosage/structure and lasting generational outcomes remain unknown. The draft 2027 Student Ministry leadership goal proposes a coached path, not adopted policy. Southbrook's documented current-state analysis says actual coaching frequency/quality/observation/fidelity have not been verified locally.

## What was executed
- Applied main-branch original PROMPT_ONLY and the proposed V2 PROMPT_ONLY to exactly this question; wrote two separate copyable prompts.
- Used identical nine public source URLs (programmatically verified identical sets): Kraft 2018; Glover 2023; Kluger & DeNisi 1996; IES/ERIC 2022; Mercy Hill, Relevant Community apprentice checklist, Relevant Community coach guide, InterVarsity, NIST TWI.
- Created two bounded standalone research drafts from those prompt instructions, **in the same session with the same assistant**. No fresh independent models, randomized sequence, blinded grader, or repeated runs.
- Used original publisher/institution/ERIC abstracts for the first four empirical sources and full primary organization practice pages for the others. The draft reports distinguish academic education mechanisms from claimed church outcomes.
- Programmatic artifact QA: **10/10 static output checks**, including same source packet/ledgers, required prompt sections, all cited IDs, no generational causal promotion, and honest limits.
- One **subjective, unblinded** rubric assessment of the prompts: original 9/12, candidate 12/12, chiefly because V2 demands direct premise scrutiny, steelmanned volunteer objection, reversal criteria, and substantive conclusion revision. This number is not evidence of reliability.
- Candidate text is longer: original prompt 585 words vs V2 738 (+26%); bounded research drafts 2,149 vs 2,494 (+16%). More complete self-challenge can also create word/usage overhead and anchor a false 'less is always better' conclusion.

## Observed learning
Both report drafts are cautious, source-traceable and practical. V2's distinctive conclusion is not that minimal coaching always wins, but: **use the least burdensome level of coaching that produces repeatable observed task capability while maintaining appropriate oversight; expand support when risk, performance, or delivery warrants.** That is a local design *hypothesis*, not an established effect estimate.

No independent source in this bounded packet establishes spiritual maturity, enduring disciple-making relationships, ideal volunteer coaching dose, or G2/G3 multiplication; published church procedures are not controlled comparisons. No verified Brain confidence upgrade or ministry-policy change.
